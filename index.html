La Nostra Storia

Our Story

Welcome to Olivetto, a warm and inviting Italian restaurant on Sunderland Road, South Shields, serving freshly prepared food inspired by the heart of Italy and the Mediterranean.

At Olivetto, our focus is simple: good food, welcoming service, and a relaxed dining experience where every guest feels at home. From wood-fired style pizzas and handmade pasta dishes to char-grilled steaks, seafood, chicken parmos, burgers and fresh lunch options, our menu brings together classic Italian flavours with comforting local favourites.

Whether you are joining us for a family meal, a romantic dinner, a casual lunch, or a special celebration, Olivetto offers a cosy atmosphere where food is made to be enjoyed and shared.

Our kitchen prepares dishes with care, using fresh ingredients, rich sauces, traditional flavours and generous portions. Every plate is created with the aim of giving our guests a memorable dining experience from the first bite to the last.

Il Cibo

Our Food

At Olivetto, food is at the centre of everything we do.

Our menu is designed to offer something for everyone, from light lunches and fresh crispy salads to rich pasta dishes, stone-style pizzas, seafood, grilled meats, chicken parmos and homemade burgers.

Guests can enjoy Italian classics such as Spaghetti Bolognese, Tagliatelle Carbonara, Lasagne, Penne Alfredo, Risotto, Seafood Linguine and Margherita Pizza, alongside house favourites including Chicken Parmo, Sirloin Diane, Fillet al Pepe, Sea Bass, Salmon Pescatora and Tower Burger.

We also offer a popular Early Bird Menu, giving guests the perfect opportunity to enjoy a starter, pizza or pasta, and a hot drink at excellent value.

Every dish is prepared with care using fresh ingredients, bold flavours and generous portions. Whether you are craving pasta, pizza, seafood, steak, chicken or a relaxed lunch, Olivetto has something to suit every appetite.

Il Ristorante

The Restaurant

Olivetto offers a relaxed and welcoming dining experience in the heart of South Shields.

Our restaurant is perfect for casual meals, date nights, family gatherings, birthdays, business lunches and group bookings. The warm interior, friendly service and carefully prepared menu create the ideal setting for both everyday dining and special occasions.

Whether you are visiting for a quick lunch, an early evening meal, a weekend dinner or a Sunday lunch, our team is here to make you feel welcome from the moment you arrive.
